Training load

How much you ran and trained each week. Distance and cross-training come from Garmin; Stryd's Running Stress Score (RSS) weighs each run by its power.

Fitness

Critical Power (CP) is the highest power you could hold for roughly 40–60 minutes. Efficiency is how much power you produce per heartbeat on steady runs — up means fitter.

Recovery

Garmin's overnight and all-day signals, as 7-day averages. A rising resting heart rate, falling Body Battery and short sleep together usually mean fatigue. Dashed lines mark marathon days.

Running form

Stryd's Form Profile scores (Elastic Power, Landing Control, Shock Protection, Rhythm and Forward Drive) are only available in the Stryd app, so each card tracks the Stryd measurement closest to that attribute. To keep pace changes from looking like form changes, only steady runs are used: at least 20 minutes at 65–85% of your CP at the time, and not races. Tiles compare your last 3 months with the same 3 months a year earlier.

Form data starts on 14 February 2025. Before then, leg spring stiffness and vertical oscillation were recorded on a different scale (about 50% and 25% higher), which looks like a change in the pod's calibration rather than in your running.

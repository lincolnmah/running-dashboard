Training load

How much you ran and trained each week. Distance and cross-training come from Garmin; Stryd's Running Stress Score (RSS) weighs each run by its power.

Fitness

Critical Power (CP) is the highest power you could hold for roughly 40–60 minutes. Efficiency is how much power you produce per heartbeat on steady runs — up means fitter.

Recovery

Garmin's overnight and all-day signals, as 7-day averages. A rising resting heart rate, falling Body Battery and short sleep together usually mean fatigue. Dashed lines mark marathon days.

Marathons

Every marathon in your Stryd history. %CP is race power as a share of your CP on the day; around 80–85% is typical for a marathon.
